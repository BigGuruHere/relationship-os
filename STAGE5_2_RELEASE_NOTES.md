# Stage 5.2 Quality Fixes

This package builds on Stage 5.1 and focuses on usability, data hygiene, and CRM search quality.

Included:
- Back button on enrichment options pages for contacts and companies.
- Task focus field: Doing now, Not doing now, Never doing now.
- Task inbox ordering and quick focus updates.
- Company phone number field.
- Company tags and company tag search.
- Project notes with voice/text capture and summary storage.
- Project task title links to task edit.
- Improved search for partial contact names, phone numbers, company phone, and company tags.
- Duplicate checks for contact name, contact phone, company name, deal title, and project title.
- Contact page now displays form errors for Want/Offer save failures.
- Company enrichment can apply company phone field-level updates.

Deferred to Stage 6+
- Lead entity and lead conversion flows.
- Buyer mandate / seller opportunity views.
- Market Map view.
- Deeper graph/linking overhaul across Projects, Deals, Wants, Offers, Companies, Contacts, and Leads.
- Full project page collapse/expand redesign.
- Full note indexing/semantic search for every note type.
