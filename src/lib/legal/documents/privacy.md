### 1. Our commitment to privacy

Dorian Kreed and Relish are designed to help people develop meaningful and valuable relationships with other people.

To do this, our Services may collect and maintain information about participants' circumstances, interests, aspirations, capabilities, needs and relationships.

We recognise that this information can be deeply personal.

This Privacy Policy explains how Value Group Pty Ltd collects, holds, uses, discloses, secures and manages personal information.

Our objective is to provide services of value while respecting privacy and providing meaningful control over participation and disclosure.

### 3. How Dorian and Relish use information

Dorian communicates with participants through supported voice, text and digital channels.

Relish maintains and processes information to support continuity, personalised interactions, relationship management and relevant human connections.

Information may be organised, summarised, analysed and interpreted by automated systems.

Information shared with Dorian is not held exclusively by Dorian. It may be processed by Value Group and authorised service providers.

### 4. Information we collect

Depending on your use of the Services, we may collect:

**Identity and contact information:** names, preferred names, telephone numbers, email addresses, account identifiers and verification information.

**Communications:** conversation transcripts, SMS messages, summaries, instructions, invitations and introduction histories.

**Personal and relationship information:** interests, goals, aspirations, personal circumstances, relationships, professional experience, capabilities, contributions, needs and preferences.

**Generated information:** AI-generated summaries, inferences, interpretations, potential connection indicators and contextual information.

**Technical information:** call metadata, network and device information, service logs, diagnostic information, security events and records of permissions and consent.

We seek to limit collection to information reasonably necessary for the Services and other lawful, disclosed purposes.

### 5. Sensitive information

Participants may disclose sensitive information, including information about health, beliefs and other highly personal circumstances.

We do not require participants to disclose every aspect of their personal lives.

Where sensitive information is collected, we will comply with applicable consent and other legal requirements.

We may restrict unnecessary collection, use or disclosure of sensitive information and apply additional safeguards.

### 6. How we obtain information

Information may be collected directly from you, through your conversations, messages, profile updates and other interactions.

We may also receive information from authorised sources or from other participants who discuss their own relationships and experiences.

Information may be generated through automated processing of information legitimately available to the Services.

When information about someone is received from another person, we may preserve the perspective and context in which it was provided.

### 7. Purposes for which information is used

We use information to:

- Operate Dorian and Relish.
- Establish and maintain accounts and conversation continuity.
- Personalise interactions and support participant needs.
- Manage relationships, interests, goals and opportunities.
- Provide optional relationship matching.
- Facilitate permitted introductions and communications.
- Manage sharing permissions and participant choices.
- Provide support and resolve technical issues.
- Protect security and investigate misuse.
- Assess reliability, troubleshoot issues and improve service quality.
- Meet legal obligations.

We will obtain consent or rely on another lawful basis where necessary for a particular activity.

### 8. Calls, audio and transcripts

Dorian may use live voice processing to conduct telephone conversations.

**Value Group's intended policy is not to retain routine call audio recordings.**

Voice processing may nevertheless involve temporary handling of audio by telecommunications and AI providers.

Conversation transcripts may be generated and retained to enable continuity and the relationship management functions of Relish.

Transcripts may include sensitive or highly personal information.

Some providers may independently retain technical data or conversation information under their applicable configurations and contractual arrangements.

Value Group will confirm its recording and provider-retention settings before publishing this Policy as final.

### 9. Personalisation and ongoing understanding

Dorian may use relevant information from previous interactions to provide more personalised support over time.

This information may include statements provided by participants, summaries, contextual interpretations and automated inferences.

Inferences may be incomplete or incorrect and are not necessarily independently verified facts.

We may update or correct information as new information becomes available.

### 10. Information about other people

Participants may discuss friends, relatives, colleagues, professional contacts and others.

We may retain this information in the context in which it was provided.

Our systems may distinguish between:

- Information someone provides about themselves.
- An opinion or statement another person expresses about them.
- Information inferred by automated systems.

One person's statement about another is not automatically treated as verified information provided by the person concerned.

Information may still legally constitute personal information about an individual even when it is held in another participant's account.

We will manage access, correction, retention and disclosure consistently with applicable privacy obligations and the rights of affected people.

### 11. Optional relationship matching

Relish may use information to identify potentially valuable relationships and opportunities.

Matching may consider interests, goals, needs, capabilities, circumstances and opportunities for mutual contribution.

**Participation in matching is optional.**

You may choose not to have your personal data used for cross-participant relationship matching.

When matching is disabled, your information may continue to be processed to provide your other enabled Dorian services, but will not be used for optional cross-participant matching.

When matching is enabled, relevant information may be considered internally to identify possible connections, subject to applicable privacy safeguards.

Enabling matching does not automatically authorise public disclosure of your information.

You may subsequently disable matching. This will apply to future matching activity, subject to technical implementation and lawful exceptions.

Disabling matching does not automatically delete your account information or retract information already lawfully disclosed.

### 12. Introductions and sharing

We may suggest or facilitate introductions where permitted.

A potential connection may be identified internally without revealing a participant's identity or private details to another participant.

Where an introduction involves disclosure, the applicable permissions, participant choices and legal requirements determine what may be communicated.

An invitation or QR code does not automatically entitle the inviting person to access your private conversations, transcripts, telephone number or personal data.

We aim to limit disclosure to information reasonably necessary for an authorised purpose.

Once information has been lawfully shared with another person, their independent use of that information may be outside our control.

### 13. Organisations and networks

Relish may support organisational, professional and community relationship networks.

Membership of, or an invitation from, an organisation does not automatically entitle it to access a participant's private Dorian conversations or all personal information associated with that participant.

Information within shared or organisational environments is governed by relevant purposes, permissions and legal obligations.

Additional notices may apply to future organisational services.

### 14. Internal service improvement

Value Group may use information to operate, monitor, evaluate, maintain and improve Dorian and Relish.

This may include:

- Diagnosing errors and technical failures.
- Assessing AI response quality and accuracy.
- Investigating incorrect summaries or interpretations.
- Testing conversation flows and service reliability.
- Evaluating relationship recommendations.
- Improving system prompts, procedures and processing logic.
- Developing and validating product capabilities.

Where permitted by law and appropriate to the purpose, this work may involve controlled review of identifiable transcripts or other personal information by authorised personnel.

We will apply access restrictions, data minimisation, confidentiality and other appropriate safeguards.

We will use de-identified or aggregated information where reasonably practicable and suitable for the purpose.

This section does not authorise unrestricted use of identifiable personal information for AI model training or disclosure to unrelated third parties.

### 15. AI model training

We distinguish between operating or improving our Services and training or fine-tuning AI models using identifiable personal information.

Where identifiable personal information is proposed to be used for training or fine-tuning AI models, we will assess the applicable legal requirements and the nature of that use.

We will seek separate informed consent where required, including where consent is necessary because of the nature of the information or the purpose of processing.

We will not treat general acceptance of this Privacy Policy as blanket permission for unrestricted identifiable model training.

Where available, participants may be offered specific choices about optional model-training uses.

Third-party AI providers' processing and training arrangements are subject to the relevant contracts and configurations, which we review as part of our privacy management practices.

Value Group takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

### 20. Retention

We retain personal information while reasonably needed for its permitted purposes, subject to legal obligations.

Our intended policy is:

| Information | Intended retention |
|---|---|
| Routine call audio | Not stored |
| Conversation transcripts | While the account remains active, subject to deletion requests and lawful exceptions |
| Personal and relationship information | While needed to provide the Services or for another lawful purpose |
| Operational SMS content | Generally no longer than 90 days in systems we control, subject to verified provider arrangements and exceptions |
| Diagnostic and security logs | Generally 30-180 days |
| Consent and acceptance history | As reasonably necessary for compliance and dispute resolution |
| Backups | Until the relevant backup retention cycle expires |

Information no longer required will be deleted or de-identified where required by law.

Deletion from active systems may occur before deletion from backups or provider systems.

Final retention periods will be verified against production configurations.

### 21. Access to your personal information

You may request access to personal information held about you.

We will consider access requests in accordance with applicable Australian privacy law.

Legal access rights may extend to opinions, automated inferences and other personal information maintained within our systems.

Access rights do not necessarily require disclosure of proprietary software, algorithms, database architecture or other intellectual property.

We will not refuse access to personal information solely because it is held within proprietary technology.

We may verify your identity before providing information.

We aim to respond within 30 calendar days where practicable and will comply with any applicable legal timeframe.

### 22. Correction

You may request correction of personal information that is inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, irrelevant or misleading.

We will assess such requests in accordance with law.

Where information is another person's opinion, appropriate correction may involve identifying its source, qualifying the statement or associating a correction, rather than falsely representing that the opinion was never expressed.

### 23. Deletion and account closure

You may request deletion of information or closure of your account by contacting us.

We will delete or de-identify information where legally required and where otherwise appropriate under our published commitments.

Some information may need to be retained for lawful purposes including security, legal obligations or dispute resolution.

Information already lawfully disclosed to independent recipients may not be recoverable by Value Group.

Deletion from backups or provider systems may follow their verified deletion processes.
